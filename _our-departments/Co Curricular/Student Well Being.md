<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">

<p><strong>Peer Support Programme</strong>
</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>The Peer Support Programme fosters a caring school culture and strengthens
a network of positive relationships. Through structured lessons, students
develop social-emotional skills and build strong peer support through meaningful
interactions with their peers. Initiatives, such as the ‘Be A Buddy’ program
for incoming Primary 1s and our International Buddy Programme, ensure every
new student feels welcomed and quickly adjusts to school life.</p>

<p><strong>Build Flourishing Friendships</strong>
</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>Our Build Flourishing Friendships (BFF) programme is designed to nurture
strong friendships, positive relationships, and values-driven behaviours
in our students.</p>
<p>Through BFF, students learn and practise key values such as respect, compassion,
integrity, self-discipline, resilience, and harmony. These are brought
to life through engaging stories, meaningful class discussions, and everyday
interactions, helping students make sense of real-life situations.</p>
<p>A distinctive feature of the programme is our “Garden Helpers”—six lovable
insect characters that serve as visual anchors for each value. By encountering
these familiar characters across the school, students are reminded of how
they can respond with care, responsibility, and thoughtfulness in their
daily lives.</p>

<p><strong>Cyberwellness</strong>
</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>Our Cyber Wellness education empowers students to become savvy, e-learning-ready
technology users. We guide them to be discerning netizens who spread positivity
and uphold truth, justice, and love in both digital and physical spaces.
Ultimately, we aim to develop Women of Grace and Substance who navigate
the 21st-century technological landscape with integrity and character.</p>

<p><strong>Educational Career Guidance</strong>
</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>Our Education and Career Guidance (ECG) Programme aims to empower our
girls to make informed, responsible decisions as they discover their unique
potential. Through annual Educational Career Guidance fair and insights
from inspiring speakers, we build the awareness and motivate our students
to pursue their aspirations with confidence.</p>
