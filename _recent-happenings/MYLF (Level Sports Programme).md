<p>The Level Sports Programme held during the Mid-Year Learning Festival
aims to provide students with meaningful opportunities to explore new interests,
develop confidence, and build important life skills beyond the classroom.
Through engaging and hands-on experiences, students are encouraged to develop
resilience, focus, teamwork, and self-discipline while discovering their
individual strengths and talents.</p>
<p>This year, our students experienced Laser Target Shooting for Primary
1, Wushu for Primary 2, and Archery for Primary 4. Through these programmes,
students developed important skills such as focus, coordination, discipline,
perseverance, and teamwork, while also building physical fitness and confidence.
The activities supported holistic development by providing opportunities
for students to demonstrate sportsmanship, collaborate with their peers,
and appreciate new experiences.</p>
<p>Through exposure to different sports, we hope this would encourage our
girls to continue to pursue active lifestyles and personal growth.</p>
