<p>As one CHIJ (Katong) Primary family, we came together on Friday 29 May
to celebrate Founder’s Day in faith, joy and thanksgiving.</p>
<p>From the Eucharistic Mass and meaningful Procession of Gifts to the lively
class activities and Recess Fiesta lovingly helmed by our Parent Volunteers,
the day beautifully reflected our IJ spirit of compassion, unity and service.
Our Gift of Love outreach also reminded us of the importance of caring
for those in need with generous hearts.</p>
<p>Inspired by Blessed Nicolas Barré’s call to <em>“Do all through love, nothing through constraint,”</em> our
girls continue to grow <em>From Girls to Women of Grace &amp; Substance </em>and
Ambassadors of peace who care for one another and reach out to the poor
with love and generosity.</p>
<p><strong>Strong As One</strong>, we journey on together, rooted always
in Truth, Justice, Freedom and Love.</p>
