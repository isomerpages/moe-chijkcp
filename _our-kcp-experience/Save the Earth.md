<h4>Saving Our Earth - Together</h4>
<p><strong>Our Mission:</strong>
</p>
<p>Our mission is to educate, inspire, and empower children to protect Earth's
environment.</p>
<p>At CHIJ (Katong) Primary, we believe that every one of us has a responsibility
to care for the planet we call home. Our mission is simple but powerful:
to educate, inspire, and empower our students to protect Earth's environment
— one small action at a time.</p>
<p>Through our programmes, students discover the importance of recycling,
managing food waste, and separating trash the right way. But learning doesn't
stop at the school gate. We encourage our students to bring these habits
home, making sustainability a way of life for their families and communities
too.</p>
<p>We want every child at CHIJ (Katong) Primary to feel proud to be part
of a school that truly cares — about the environment, about the future,
and about the world they will inherit. Because when young people understand
the impact of their choices, they become the changemakers our planet needs.</p>

<p><u>Composting of food scraps</u>
</p>
<p>Canteen vendors deposit their food waste into containers provided at each
stall. These containers are collected daily and the waste is processed
in a composting machine. The resulting compost is then used as fertiliser
for our school gardens. On average, about 4 - 5 kg of compost is produced
each term.</p>
